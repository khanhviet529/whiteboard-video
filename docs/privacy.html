Privacy Policy

Whiteboard Video Uploader · last updated 21 August 2026

Summary: the Software runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, collects nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere except directly to TikTok's own API. The author receives no data of any kind.

1. Who this applies to

This policy describes what Whiteboard Video Uploader (“the Software”) does with data when you install and run it on your own computer. There is no hosted service, so there is no operator collecting data about visitors or users.

2. What the Software stores, and where

DataWhere it is storedWhy
TikTok client key and client secret dang/.env on your computer Required to start TikTok's OAuth flow
Access token, refresh token, your TikTok open_id, granted scopes dang/token.json on your computer To upload without asking you to log in again; the token is refreshed automatically
A record of what you uploaded: file path, content hash, TikTok publish_id, title, timestamp dang/da-dang.json on your computer So the same video is never uploaded twice by mistake
Video files and thumbnails out/ and build/ on your computer They are the videos you produced

All of these files stay on your computer. None of them are uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to the author or any third party. Two of them (.env and token.json) are excluded from the project's version control so they cannot be published by accident.

3. What is sent over the network, and to whom

The Software makes network requests to exactly one destination: open.tiktokapis.com and www.tiktok.com — TikTok's own servers. Those requests are:

  • Exchanging your authorisation code for tokens, and refreshing them.
  • Reading your own creator settings before an upload, as TikTok requires.
  • Uploading your video file.
  • Reading the view, like, comment, and share counts of your own posts.

There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no requests to any other host. Your access token is sent only to TikTok, in the Authorization header, and is never written to the Software's logs.

4. Data about other people

The Software requests only scopes that concern your own account (video.upload, video.publish, video.list, user.info.basic). It does not read, store, or transmit information about other TikTok users, their videos, their comments, or their profiles.

5. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Files remain on your computer until you delete them. To remove everything the Software stored:

  • Delete dang/token.json — this revokes nothing at TikTok's end but removes the stored credentials from your machine.
  • Delete dang/.env and dang/da-dang.json.
  • Remove the app's access to your TikTok account in TikTok's own settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). This is what actually invalidates the tokens.

Content you have already published on TikTok is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy, not by this one. Delete such posts in the TikTok app.

6. AI-generated content

Videos produced by the Software contain a synthesised Vietnamese voice-over generated by a local text-to-speech model. No voice recording of any real person is collected or transmitted. Every upload sets TikTok's is_aigc flag to disclose that the content is AI-generated.

7. Children

The Software is a developer tool and is not directed at children.
